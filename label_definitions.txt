ANALYSIS: Makes an argument backed by at least one specific, checkable fact (box office numbers, release dates, production details, named crew/cast, critical scores, specific plot points, technical details like runtime or budget).
HOT_TAKE: A confident claim or judgment with NO specific supporting evidence. May be true or insightful, but isn't argued - just stated. Typically uses strong language ("best," "worst," "masterpiece," "garbage") without backing it up.
DISCUSSION: Explicitly invites others to weigh in. May ask a question, request recommendations, seek interpretation help, or present multiple viewpoints and ask which is right. The PURPOSE is conversation, not declaration.
